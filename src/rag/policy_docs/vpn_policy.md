# VPN Access Policy

**Policy Category:** VPN
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-01

## Eligibility

All full-time and contract employees are eligible for VPN access once their manager approves the request through the internal access portal. VPN access is not granted automatically upon hire; employees must submit a request during their onboarding week.

## Setup Process

1. Employee requests VPN access via the IT self-service portal or by contacting IT Support.
2. Manager approval is required before provisioning.
3. IT Support provisions a VPN profile within 1 business day of approval and emails setup instructions to the employee's corporate address.
4. Employees install the GlobalTech VPN client and authenticate using their standard corporate credentials plus multi-factor authentication (MFA).

## Renewal and Expiry

VPN access expires automatically after 90 days of inactivity. Employees who have not connected in 90 days must re-request access through their manager. Active users are not affected by this expiry.

## Common Issues

- **Cannot connect:** Confirm MFA is set up correctly and the VPN client is updated to the latest version. If the issue persists after these checks, a support ticket should be created.
- **Slow connection:** Usually caused by connecting to a distant regional gateway. Employees should select the VPN gateway closest to their physical location.
- **Access revoked unexpectedly:** Typically due to the 90-day inactivity expiry; re-request access via the portal.

## Restrictions

VPN access is for business use only. Split-tunneling is disabled by default and may only be enabled by IT Support for approved business reasons. VPN credentials must never be shared between employees.
