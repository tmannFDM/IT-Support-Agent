# Software Licensing Policy

**Policy Category:** Software
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-28

## Approved Software Catalog

GlobalTech maintains an approved software catalog available through the internal software portal. Employees may self-install any catalog item without additional approval, including standard productivity, communication, and development tools.

## Requesting Non-Catalog Software

Software not in the approved catalog requires a request through IT Support including:

- Name and vendor of the software
- Business justification
- Manager approval

Requests are reviewed by IT Security for license compliance and data-handling risk before approval. Typical turnaround is 5 business days.

## License Types and Costs

- **Per-seat licenses** (e.g. design or analytics tools) are assigned to a single employee and must be returned to the license pool when no longer needed.
- **Floating licenses** are shared across a team and checked out for the duration of use.
- Departments are billed for licenses assigned to their employees; cost approval is the requesting manager's responsibility.

## Renewal and Deactivation

Licenses are reviewed quarterly. Unused licenses (no activity in 60 days) may be reclaimed and reassigned. Employees who need continued access should confirm usage during the quarterly review notice.

## Prohibited Software

Employees may not install software from outside the approved catalog or unauthorized vendor channels without prior IT Security approval, including free/trial tools, browser extensions with broad permissions, and personal cloud storage clients not on the approved list.
