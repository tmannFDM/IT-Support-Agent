# Hardware Request Policy

**Policy Category:** Hardware
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-20

## Standard Equipment

All employees are issued a standard laptop, charger, and headset upon hire as part of onboarding. Standard equipment does not require a separate request.

## Requesting Additional or Replacement Hardware

Employees who need additional hardware (a second monitor, docking station, mouse, keyboard, or similar) or a replacement for damaged/lost equipment must submit a hardware request ticket including:

- The specific item requested
- Business justification (for additional equipment) or a description of the damage/loss (for replacements)
- Manager approval for any request over the standard equipment allowance

## Approval and Fulfillment Timelines

- Standard replacement items (charger, mouse, keyboard): approved automatically, shipped within 3 business days.
- Additional equipment (monitors, docking stations): requires manager approval, typically fulfilled within 5–7 business days after approval.
- Laptop replacements due to damage or loss: require manager approval and an IT security review before reissue, typically 5–10 business days.

## Loss or Theft

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Support immediately so the device can be remotely locked or wiped. A police report may be required for stolen equipment insurance claims over $1,000 in value.

## Return of Equipment

All company hardware must be returned to IT within 5 business days of an employee's last day, using the prepaid shipping label provided by HR.
