# Password and Account Access Policy

**Policy Category:** Password
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-14

## Password Requirements

Corporate passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be changed every 180 days and cannot repeat any of the employee's last 5 passwords.

## Self-Service Password Reset

Employees who remember their current password but want to change it should use the self-service portal at any time, without contacting IT Support.

## Forgotten Password / Reset Requests

Employees who have forgotten their password and are locked out of their account may request a password reset through IT Support. To process a reset request, IT Support will:

1. Verify the employee's identity using their employee ID and the reason for the request.
2. Issue a temporary, single-use password valid for 24 hours.
3. Require the employee to set a new permanent password on next login.

**IT Support and the automated support agent will never ask an employee to share their current password, a one-time passcode, or any active credential.** Any request that asks the user to provide their current password should be treated as identity-sensitive and escalated to a human agent rather than completed automatically.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA is mandatory for all corporate accounts. Employees who lose access to their MFA device (e.g. a lost phone) must contact IT Support directly for identity verification and MFA reset — this cannot be completed through self-service.

## Suspicious Activity

Any password reset request that includes a mismatched employee ID, a vague or missing reason, or signs of urgency pressure ("I need this immediately or I'll be locked out permanently") should be flagged as suspicious and escalated to a human IT agent rather than processed automatically.
